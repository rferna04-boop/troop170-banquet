Why We Gather: The Revival Experience

Whether you are sleeping under the pine canopy or raising a glass with old friends at the dinner table, this revival was crafted for you.

“Celebrate Scouting by Scouting”

We believe the truest celebration of Troop 170 isn’t spent sitting in a generic function hall—it lives around campfires, in heated rustic cabins, over Dutch ovens in the crisp December air, and across shared dinner tables with the people who make this unit a family.

🌲 Youth Adventures • Bunks • Outdoor Skills

For Scouts, AOLs & Webelos

An action-packed December weekend designed to test your trail skills, ignite your leadership, and forge lasting brotherhood between Troops and Packs.

  • ✓
    Heated Cabin Life: Stay warm in historic camp cabins with bunkmates, nighttime camaraderie, and cracker barrel treats.
  • ✓
    Pioneering & Wilderness Skills: Hands-on fire building, winter navigation, knots, and cold-weather camping mastery.
  • ✓
    Charcoal Dutch Oven Apple Pies: Master outdoor baking techniques with piping-hot, scratch-made cinnamon desserts over glowing coals.
  • ✓
    Fireside Mentorship: Webelos & AOL Scouts experience Troop 170’s scout-led culture firsthand alongside seasoned Senior Patrol Leaders.
🦃 Community Heritage • Tradition • Feast

For Alumni, Families & Friends

A heartwarming homecoming honoring six decades of Troop 170 history, community service, Eagle achievements, and unforgettable memories.

  • ✓
    Scratch-Made Roast Turkey Feast: Traditional roasted turkey dinner, savory stuffing, warm mashed potatoes, homemade gravy, and pies.
  • ✓
    Traditional Scout Skits & Songs: Laugh along with legendary patrol skits, hilarious campfire performances, and musical traditions.
  • ✓
    Court of Honor & Leadership Awards: Stand together to cheer rank advancements, merit badges, and Eagle Scout milestones.
  • ✓
    Generational Fellowship: Reconnect with fellow parents, Scouters, past Scoutmasters, and alumni who built this troop.

Venue Directions & Logistics

Everything you need to know about getting to Camp Workcoeman and preparing for the weekend.

Address

Camp Workcoeman
169 Camp Workcoeman Rd, New Hartford, CT 06057

Dress Code

Scouts & Scouters: Full Class A Field Uniform.
Families, Alumni & Guests: Seasonal festive or neat casual attire with comfortable warm footwear for camp walkways.

Parking Instructions

Park in the main lower parking lot by the camp gate. Carpooling is encouraged. Parking attendants will be on hand to assist arriving families.

Accommodations & Warmth

The Dining Hall and AOL Cabins are heated. Bring warm layers, winter coats, flashlights, and sturdy boots for evening paths.
