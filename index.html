🦅 The Trail to Eagle

A Tradition of Service and Character

Achieved by only four percent of all Scouts, the rank of Eagle represents years of disciplined effort. In 2026, Troop 170 proudly presents six extraordinary leaders who led over 800 hours of community impact projects.

🦅

Alex Rodriguez

Senior Patrol Leader • Age 17
Project: Nature Reserve Wetland Boardwalk Engineered 250 feet of all-weather elevated trail boardwalk and native root plantings to halt wetland erosion at Miller Nature Reserve.
🦅

Liam Chen

Troop Scribe & Guide • Age 16
Project: Elementary Outdoor Reading Pavilion Designed and erected dual weather-resistant mini outdoor libraries with sensory literacy benches for Oakridge Elementary.
🦅

Marcus Miller

Quartermaster • Age 17
Project: Veterans Memorial Grave Staging Manufactured 12 bronze ceremonial flag deposit bins and cataloged 420 veteran gravesites with GPS beacons for Memorial Day.
🦅

Benjamin Foster

Assistant Senior Patrol Leader • Age 17
Project: Arboretum Pollinator Sanctuary Installed 6 bee nesting hotels, 10 bat sanctuaries, and 1,000 square feet of drought-tolerant wildflowers at the County Arboretum.
🦅

Ethan Wright

Junior Assistant Scoutmaster • Age 18
Project: Food Pantry Cold Storage & Access Constructed ADA-compliant ingress ramps and heavy-duty cold storage sorting bays for the Saint Jude Community Food Pantry.
🦅

Samuel Davis

Bugler & Instructor • Age 16
Project: Fire Dept Rapid-Deploy Lockers Fabricated modular, quick-deployment emergency medical lockers and rescue equipment racks for Fire Engine Station 4.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details regarding ceremonial protocol, dress code, parking, and logistics.

What is the dress code for Scouts and guests?
For Youth Scouts: Full Class A (Field) Uniform is mandatory. Merit badge sashes and formal neckerchiefs should be worn for the official Court of Honor portraiture.
For Parents & Guests: Business casual or semi-formal gala attire is customary for this celebratory milestone.
May extended family, grandparents, and siblings attend?
Yes, gladly. The Annual Banquet is our premier community fellowship event. Grandparents, relatives, and siblings are warmly welcomed. Toddlers under five attend compliments of the Troop.
How does settlement via Scout Account / Scout Bucks work?
Scouts who have earned funds through product sales or service fundraisers may allocate their internal Troop balance toward gala tickets. Select "Deduct from Scout Account" during RSVP, and our Troop Scribe will confirm your balance.
How are dietary restrictions and allergens accommodated?
Our culinary team accommodates vegetarian, vegan, celiac / gluten-free, nut-free, and halal requirements. Please describe any specific accommodations in the designated field on the reservation form.
How will the Silent Auction bidding conclude?
Pledges may be registered online in advance or in person at the display tables during the 5:30 PM reception. Bidding closes sharply at 8:45 PM. Winning bidders may settle via Cash, Check, or Zelle.
