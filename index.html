Schedule & Program Agenda

A structured breakdown of the weekend campout and Saturday evening banquet.

Saturday Evening Banquet (Dec 5)

4:00 PM

Guest Arrival & Fellowship Hour

Check-in at the Dining Hall, cider & appetizers, viewing year-in-review photo gallery.

5:00 PM

Opening Ceremony & Invocations

Presentation of the colors, senior leadership welcome, and table blessings.

5:15 PM

Thanksgiving-Style Feast

Full roasted turkey dinner, scratch-made sides, hot carving stations, and dessert buffet.

6:30 PM

Year-End Court of Honor & Awards

Rank advancements, merit badges, Scout of the Year recognition, and leadership acknowledgments.

7:45 PM

Closing Scoutmaster's Minute & Retire Colors

Adjournment for day guests; evening campfire for weekend campers.

Weekend Campout Timeline (Dec 4–6)

Fri 6:00 PM

Troop Check-In & Cabin/Tenting Setup

Gear haul, cabin bunk assignments, safety orientation, and cracker barrel.

Sat 9:00 AM

Outdoor Skills & AOL Bridging Activities

Orienteering, cold-weather camping demonstration, and patrol cooking challenges.

Sat 2:00 PM

Kitchen Prep & Hall Setup

Volunteers and Scouts assist with dining hall setup and table dressing.

Sat 4:00 PM

Banquet & Celebration (All Units)

Join families, alumni, and Webelos packs in the main dining hall.

Sun 10:00 AM

Camp Clean-up & Dismissal

Leave No Trace sweep, cabin inspection, and weekend closing.

Venue & Logistics

Everything you need to know about getting to Camp Workcoeman and preparing for the evening.

Address

Camp Workcoeman
169 Camp Workcoeman Rd, New Hartford, CT 06057

Dress Code

Scouts & Scouters: Full Class A Field Uniform.
Families, Alumni & Guests: Seasonal festive or neat casual attire with comfortable warm footwear for walking between parking and the hall.

Parking Instructions

Park in the main lower parking lot by the camp gate. Carpooling is strongly encouraged. Designated parking attendants will guide overflow vehicles.

Weather & Cabin Accommodations

The Dining Hall and AOL Cabins are heated. Bring warm jackets, flashlights, and sturdy boots for navigating camp trails at dusk.
