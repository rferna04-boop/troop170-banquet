Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about attendance, dress code, parking, and logistics.

What is the dress code for Scouts and guests?
For Youth Scouts: Full Class A (Field) Uniform is required. Please ensure merit badge sashes and rank neckerchiefs are worn for the formal Court of Honor photos.
For Parents & Guests: Business casual or semi-formal attire is customary.
Can extended family, grandparents, and younger siblings attend?
Absolutely! The Annual Banquet is our premier family event of the year. Grandparents, aunts, uncles, and siblings are heartily welcomed. Children under 5 attend free of charge.
How does payment via Scout Account / Scout Bucks work?
Scouts who accumulated funds through popcorn sales, flower sales, or camp fundraisers can allocate their balance towards banquet tickets. Simply select "Deduct from Scout Account" during RSVP, and the Troop Scribe will confirm your balance.
What if our family has special dietary restrictions or allergies?
Our caterer accommodates vegetarian, vegan, celiac / gluten-free, nut-free, and kosher/halal preferences. Please note the specific requirement in the designated field on the RSVP form.
How does the Silent Auction work?
Bids can be placed in advance on this website or in person on the bid sheets during the 5:30 PM reception. Bidding closes sharply at 8:45 PM. Winning bidders can settle via Cash, Check, or Zelle.
