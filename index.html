The Mission

Celebrate Scouting by Scouting

“Celebrate Scouting by Scouting”

We believe the truest celebration of Troop 170 isn’t spent sitting in a generic function hall—it lives around campfires, in heated rustic cabins, over Dutch ovens in the crisp December air, and across shared dinner tables with the people who make this unit a family.

🌲 Youth Adventures • Bunks • Outdoor Skills

For Scouts, AOLs & Webelos

An action-packed December weekend designed to test your trail skills, ignite your leadership, and forge lasting brotherhood between Troops and Packs.

  • ✓
    Heated Cabin Lodging: Sleep warm and comfortable in Camp Workcoeman’s historic heated cabins with bunkmates, evening camaraderie, and cracker barrel treats (optional winter tent camping is also available for scouts who want the outdoor challenge!).
  • ✓
    Pioneering & Wilderness Skills: Hands-on fire building, winter navigation, knots, and outdoor skill demonstrations. Scouts enjoy daytime outdoor activities while having full access to the heated main dining hall and cabins throughout the weekend.
  • ✓
    Dutch Oven Baking Demo & Tasting: Learn outdoor cast-iron cooking techniques as leaders and senior scouts demonstrate baking traditional Dutch oven apple pies over hot coals, with warm slices to sample!
  • ✓
    Fireside Mentorship: Webelos & AOL Scouts experience Troop 170’s scout-led culture firsthand alongside seasoned Senior Patrol Leaders.
🦃 Community Heritage • Tradition • Dinner

For Alumni, Families & Friends

A heartwarming homecoming honoring six decades of Troop 170 history, community service, Eagle achievements, and unforgettable memories.

  • ✓
    Scratch-Made Roast Turkey Dinner: Traditional roasted turkey dinner, savory stuffing, warm mashed potatoes, homemade gravy, and pies.
  • ✓
    Traditional Scout Skits & Songs: Laugh along with legendary patrol skits, hilarious campfire performances, and musical traditions.
  • ✓
    Scout Presentations & Troop Highlights: Enjoy inspiring youth-led presentations, patrol highlights, and stories from a memorable year of Scouting.
  • ✓
    Generational Fellowship: Reconnect with fellow parents, Scouters, past Scoutmasters, and alumni who built this troop.
