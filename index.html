The Mission

Celebrate Scouting by Scouting

For Scouts, AOLs & Webelos

Step Into the Woods & Take the Helm

This isn’t just an indoor dinner—it’s an outdoor adventure designed to give our youth a marquee event to rally behind. Arrow of Light and Webelos Scouts get front-row immersion into a Scout-led troop in action, working through outdoor skills, firecraft, and camp life alongside older youth mentors.

Waterside Cabin is officially reserved for Arrow of Light Scouts for their first overnight stay with the troop, bridging friendships before advancing.

“We didn’t know what to expect and found we had our own cabin and Scouts as ambassadors to work with our Webelos advancement requirements. From building fires to carving leather, it showed us what Scouting was all about.” — Mr. Olshefski, Committee Chair
For Families, Alumni & Friends

Pull Up a Chair to the Family Table

For decades, the Annual Banquet has been the warm hearth of the Troop 170 community. It’s where generations of Scouts, alumni, parents, and leaders gather after a long year of adventure to break bread together.

Join us Saturday evening for a scratch-cooked Thanksgiving feast, our famous charcoal-baked pies, original Scout skits, and a Court of Honor celebrating the rank achievements of our young leaders.

“Peeling so many apples that afternoon for pies somehow cooked from scratch over charcoal coals—and then watching the boys take center stage with original skits—is unforgettable.” — A Troop 170 Tradition
